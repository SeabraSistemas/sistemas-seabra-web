From the animal to the decision. Complete management for goat and sheep producers.
Dairy and meat. Goats and sheep. One single system — in the field, in real time.
For associations, independent producers and institutions

The problem your operation faces today

  • Notebooks and spreadsheets: notes get lost, data goes stale, the animal's history gets fragmented.
  • Overloaded technicians: visits without structured records, evaluations that never make it back to the producer.
  • Decisions by gut feeling: without production, reproduction or health indicators, the farm is flying blind.
  • Associations without a view of the member herd: each producer works in isolation, with no aggregated data to negotiate, research or prove value to the community.
  • Paper in the field: rain, dust, loss, rework — and zero traceability.

The solution in 4 pillars

Seabra Small Ruminants organizes everything your operation needs into four integrated fronts:

01 · OPERATIONS

Farm routine

Herd, events (birth, weaning, sale, death, abortion, culling), breeding season, daily production, milk recording, lactations, staff with permissions, notifications and financials.

02 · TECHNICAL

Field work for technicians

Dairy LTE (16 traits) and meat LTE (13 traits), body measurements, CMT, FAMACHA, body condition score — all working offline. And now LTE by photo, scored by the Seabra team.

03 · DECISIONS

Data that drives action

Segment-adaptive dashboards, cross-indicators (reproduction × production × genetics), cost per kg produced, feed ordering, AI Assistant and export to PDF, Excel and CSV.

04 · GOVERNANCE

Association management

Data isolated per association, admin panel, management of technicians and producers, aggregated analytics and an automatic discount for members.

What's new in the app

Since the last version of this material, SeabraApp has added:

LTE by photo Pro

You photograph the animal with the guided camera (pose silhouette and level on screen) and the Seabra team scores it. The evaluation comes back to the animal's profile with a "Photo evaluation" badge, also shown in the showcase.

Breeding season

Group of females, sire and period, with a color per week: each service is logged with one tap. At kidding, the app suggests the sire from the service date and warns when two are possible.

Animal transfer

Sold an animal to another producer? Generate an 8-character code and send it by WhatsApp or e-mail. The buyer enters the code and the animal shows up in their app; your record keeps the history.

Weaning and location history

Weaning is now a handling step, with automatic category change. And you can now see where each animal went and when each pen was checked.

Feed ordering and cost per kg Pro

How much of each feed to buy, in kg and in bags, for 7 days up to 1 year. For meat herds, what it costs to produce each kg of live weight, stage by stage, with targets and a sire ranking.

PDF, Excel and spreadsheets on the computer

Every list exports to PDF, Excel or CSV. On the computer, the herd becomes a spreadsheet with the columns you choose, and screens split into list and profile side by side.

Birth notification to ABCC

For goat breeders in Brazil: the app generates the Excel file ready to paste into the ABCC form and marks who has already been reported.

Milk recording report

Production histogram, production per pen and the 15 best and 15 worst animals of each recording.

Features by user role

Producer

Records what happens on the farm without complicating the daily routine.
  • Herd: registration, editing, filtered lists, a detailed individual profile per animal and, on the computer, the herd as a spreadsheet.
  • Events: births (including stillbirths), weaning, reproduction and breeding season, abortions, deaths, sales, culling, drying off, weighing, clinical records and handling.
  • Production: daily production, milk recording with report and milk shipments.
  • Lactations: complete history and details of closed lactations.
  • Milk analyses: composition, SCC, TBC, fat and protein.
  • Dashboard: production charts, herd composition and reproductive indicators.
  • Technical requests: opening and tracking technician visits.
  • Animal transfer: hands the animal over to another producer with a code, without exposing any user list.
  • Staff with permissions: up to 2 on Intermediario and unlimited on Pro, each seeing and using only what the producer allows.
  • Notifications: kiddings, pregnancy checks, weanings, analyses, visits and handling reminders in the app — and, on Pro, on WhatsApp too.
  • Financials and nutrition (Pro): expenses and revenue, cost per liter, cost per kg produced and feed ordering.
  • LTE by photo (Pro): photograph the animal and get the Seabra team's evaluation on its profile.
  • Export to PDF, Excel and CSV from every list screen.

Technician

Records evaluations and services in the field, often without a signal.
  • Linear Type Evaluation (LTE): two dedicated modules — Dairy LTE (16 traits, focused on udder, teats and dairy conformation) and Meat LTE (13 traits in 5 groups: body structure and conformation, rump and mobility, carcass and muscling, front legs and hind legs). Separate forms for males and females, total score, editable history and classification in the PDF.
  • Body measurements: heart girth, scrotal circumference and objective body measurements.
  • Visit management: schedule, farms served, activity panel.
  • Requests: receiving, approving and declining producer requests.
  • Full offline mode: LTE, measurements and visits are saved and edited without a signal and sync later.

Association Admin

Sees the association's aggregated herd and manages technicians and producers.
  • User management: registering and editing technicians and producers linked to the association.
  • Farms: overview and usage monitoring of each member farm.
  • Management analytics: global metrics, distribution by sex and by farm, aggregated production.
  • Histories: advanced filters for records, movements and reproductive events by category.
  • Centralized export: dedicated panel with date filters and aggregation by producer.
  • Member discount: 20% off each member's plan, 33% once the association passes 30 subscribers — automatic, no link or code.

Technical differentiators

01 · Offline First

Field work that doesn't depend on a signal

Local storage on each device with automatic sync and conflict resolution when the connection returns. The technician goes to the most remote farm and records LTE, measurements and visits with the usual workflow.

02 · AI Assistant

An assistant inside the app, always at hand

On Pro, it answers questions about your herd in natural language, based on the farm's real data — production, reproduction, milk quality, health, performance, genetics and much more — and also records data through conversation, in the app and on WhatsApp. On Intermediario, the assistant answers animal-science and ABCC-regulation questions, with human support on WhatsApp.

Detects scientific questions (PT/ES) and searches academic literature automatically. Knows the app manual and answers how-to questions adapted to the user's role (producer, technician, admin, staff).

03 · Export to PDF, Excel and CSV

Your data available for any external analysis

Every list screen has an export button for PDF, Excel or CSV — milk recording, handling, weighing, drying off, births, sales, deaths, abortions, culling, clinical records, milk shipments, daily production and more. On the computer, the herd becomes a spreadsheet with the columns you choose. Association admins get a centralized panel with date filters and aggregation by producer. Ready for Excel, scientific research or ERP.

04 · Per-farm configuration

The system adjusts to your productive reality

Each farm sets its own parameters: ideal weaning weight and age, weight and age for breeding entry, days to pregnancy diagnosis, standard birth weight, auto-categorization and the breeds used in registration. For associations and institutions with specific needs, we offer custom development (see commercial models below).

05 · Smart notifications + WhatsApp

The system reminds you before you need to remember

A notification center inside the app alerts you to key routine events: expected kiddings, pregnancy checks due, upcoming weanings, milk analysis results, technical visit requests, vaccinations and pending handling. On the Pro plan, the same notifications go straight to the WhatsApp of the producer, technician or staff member — no need for push, e-mail or opening the app.

06 · Staff with permissions

Your whole farm team using the system

Register your staff — feeders, milkers, office, foreman — up to 2 on Intermediario and as many as you want on Pro, at no extra cost per user. For each staff member, the producer defines exactly what they can see and use: access only to milking records, only to clinical handling, only to reproductive events, or combinations. Everyone does their part without exposing sensitive data or getting in each other's way.

07 · Multi-association and multi-platform

One platform, many associations, all devices

Each association has its own isolated data and its own admin. The same infrastructure serves many organizations with full separation. Available on Android and on the Web — on iPhone and on the computer, through the browser — on the same database. On the computer, screens split into list and profile side by side.

3 common objections and how to handle them

A spreadsheet is enough for me.
Answer: a spreadsheet doesn't warn you when a kidding is near, doesn't work offline in the field and doesn't cross indicators. Seabra does — and it still exports to Excel whenever you want.
My association has no IT team.
Answer: the system is SaaS and runs in the cloud. Nobody has to maintain a server. We support the rollout and train everyone.
The technician won't use a phone.
Answer: the app was designed for people in boots who work with animals. Simple interface, large touch targets, voice dictation, offline. On-site training available.
