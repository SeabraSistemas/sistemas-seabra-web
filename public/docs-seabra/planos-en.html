What's coming in Beef Cattle

Herd and spreadsheets

Bring in the whole herd from a spreadsheet (CSV or Excel), reviewed before saving. Filtered list, animal profile and genealogy, all the way to the semen bull.

Weighing and weight gain

Chute-side weighing at the pace of the work, average daily gain per animal and per lot, and batch operations.

Reproduction

Breeding season, palpation, FTAI and embryo transfer, with semen and tank control and protocols that remind you of the next step.

Handling and animal life cycle

Animal-by-animal handling sessions, calving, weaning with dam and calf linked, and exits by death, sale or culling.

Management and indicators

Financials, value per arroba (15 kg carcass unit) and nine indicator dashboards, exportable to CSV, Excel and PDF. Clinical records and rain gauge in the same app.

Several farms, one account

Up to 10 farms on the same subscription, animal transfers between them and staff with their own login.
